Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation uses a Firm-Fixed-Price IDIQ contract for commercial mobile telephone and data services at the U.S. Embassy in Luanda, Angola. The FFP-IDIQ structure is well-suited to these requirements. The services — 4G mobile data, cellular voice, SMS, roaming, and international calls — are commercial telecommunications offerings that carriers price at standard published rates. There is no ambiguity about what is being purchased, costs are easily estimated by comparing carrier rate plans, and the scope is stable. IDIQ accommodates the variable quantity of lines and usage across the embassy without requiring T&M or cost-reimbursement structures, since individual task orders can be placed at fixed unit prices (per-line, per-GB, per-minute, etc.). The base year plus four option periods is a standard structure for ongoing services contracts. There is no mismatch here: fixed pricing is appropriate because the market prices these services routinely, multiple providers can compete, and performance criteria (coverage, connectivity, uptime) are objectively measurable. A T&M or cost-reimbursement approach would be inappropriate and would actually harm the government by removing vendor incentive to control costs. Severity is 1 — the pricing structure is correct for this type of requirement.
