Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 3 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a commercial 50 MB intra-Europe leased telecommunications circuit, procured under FAR Part 12. The pricing structure — Monthly Recurring Charges and Non-Recurring Charges — is the industry-standard model for commercial telecom circuit leases and is effectively a firm-fixed-price arrangement. This is entirely appropriate for the requirement: the scope is well-defined (a specific bandwidth circuit between defined endpoints), the service is commercially available at market rates, costs are highly predictable, and performance is objectively measurable (circuit is either operational at specified capacity or it is not). There is no ambiguity in scope that would argue for cost-reimbursement or T&M, and no labor-hour unpredictability that would suggest T&M. Fixed recurring charges with defined NRCs for setup are the correct and conventional pricing mechanism for this type of acquisition. No square peg issue is present.
Evidence Quotes
All quoted prices shall be identified by Monthly Recurring Charge (MRC), Non-Recurring Charge (NRC), and any tiered pricing for additional service months, as applicable.
The MRC/NRC pricing structure is standard for commercial telecommunications circuit leases. This is a firm-fixed recurring charge model — the government pays a defined monthly rate for a defined bandwidth (50 MB) circuit. This is the conventional and appropriate pricing structure for commercial telecom services, which are commodity-like and predictable in cost and scope.
This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with FAR Part 12.
FAR Part 12 acquisitions are for commercial items, which are by their nature well-defined and market-priced. A commercial intra-Europe leased circuit at a specified bandwidth is a standard telecom product with established market pricing, making fixed recurring charges the correct pricing approach.
The Lowest Price Technically Acceptable (LPTA) source selection process will be used for evaluation purposes.
LPTA is consistent with the fixed-price, commodity nature of a commercial leased circuit. When requirements are well-defined (50 MB circuit, specific route, technical acceptability threshold), LPTA combined with fixed monthly pricing is appropriate and aligns incentives correctly.
