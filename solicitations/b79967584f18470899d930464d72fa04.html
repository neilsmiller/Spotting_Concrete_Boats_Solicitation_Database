Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation presents a textbook appropriate use of Firm Fixed Price contracting. The government is purchasing discrete, commercially available audio distribution hardware items from a named vendor, identified by specific part numbers and fixed quantities. There is no labor uncertainty, no evolving scope, and no technical ambiguity — the government knows exactly what it wants and how many units it needs. FFP is the correct contract type for this kind of commercial product purchase, as the price can be determined in advance, delivery is straightforward, and risk can be reasonably estimated. The simplified acquisition authority (FAR 13.106(b)(1)(i)) further supports a streamlined FFP approach for this low-complexity, supply-oriented transaction. There is no mismatch between the pricing structure and the nature of the requirement. Severity is 1 — minimal.
