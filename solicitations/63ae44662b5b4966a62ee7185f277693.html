Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for the procurement of SENTINEL Secure Chat Servers, explicitly described as commercial items. The government intends to award a Firm-Fixed Price purchase order. FFP is the appropriate contract type here: the requirement is for a defined hardware/software product (servers), commercial items have established market prices and specifications, the scope is discrete and stable (buy product X), and delivery terms can be clearly defined. There is no indication of ambiguous scope, evolving requirements, or unpredictable labor effort — all factors that would push toward T&M or cost-reimbursement. FAR 12.207 specifically requires FFP or fixed-price with economic price adjustment contracts for commercial item acquisitions, reinforcing that the chosen contract type is not only appropriate but required by regulation in this context. The solicitation does not exhibit a square peg mismatch. Severity is 1 — the contract type is well-suited to the requirement.
