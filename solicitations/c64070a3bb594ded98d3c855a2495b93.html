Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation presents a textbook case where FFP is the correct contract type. The government is purchasing a specific commercial software license (STUN Edge Node License, PN: SCT-LP SASE-CP-CLIENT, Qty 1) along with associated technical support and software maintenance from a single named vendor. The requirement is fully defined — there is a specific product, a specific part number, and a specific quantity. There is no scope uncertainty, no unpredictable labor component, and no evolving requirements. The price for a commercial software license and associated maintenance is readily estimable. FFP is explicitly recommended by the FAR for commercial product acquisitions and situations where risk can be predicted with acceptable certainty. The associated technical support and software maintenance, while potentially ongoing in nature, is bundled into a single purchase order alongside the license itself, suggesting a defined term and scope. There is no indication that the volume of support labor is unpredictable or that the work would benefit from T&M pricing. This is a well-matched contract type for the requirements described, warranting a severity of 1.
