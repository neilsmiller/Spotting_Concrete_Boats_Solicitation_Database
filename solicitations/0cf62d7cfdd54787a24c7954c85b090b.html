Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a 100MB commercial telecommunications circuit in Europe — a well-defined, commodity-type commercial service with a clear, stable scope. The requirement is not complex or uncertain: the government knows exactly what it wants (a specific bandwidth circuit between defined geographic points), and commercial carriers routinely price such services on fixed monthly recurring and one-time non-recurring charges. The pricing structure (MRC/NRC) is consistent with a fixed-price commercial services contract under FAR Part 12, which is the appropriate vehicle. There is no mismatch between the contract type and the requirements: the scope is well-defined, the deliverable is measurable (a live circuit meeting specified performance parameters), and commercial market competition will keep pricing honest. The LPTA evaluation methodology further confirms this is a straightforward price-competition for a commoditized service. No square peg concern exists here — this is precisely the kind of requirement for which fixed-price commercial contracting was designed.
