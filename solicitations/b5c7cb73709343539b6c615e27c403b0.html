Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
The solicitation uses a Firm-Fixed-Price IDIQ contract for Marine Chemist Services — specifically, gas-free certification of barge confined spaces at PSNS and NBK. This is an appropriate contract type for the requirement. Marine chemist services are a well-defined, licensed profession with standardized procedures and outputs (gas-free certificates). The service is repeatable and predictable: a certified professional visits a vessel, performs atmospheric testing, and issues or withholds certification. While the number of task orders over the 5-year ordering period may vary (hence the IDIQ structure, which appropriately handles demand uncertainty), the per-task scope is stable and estimable. FFP for each task order is sensible because: (1) the work product is a defined deliverable (certification), (2) the labor involved per engagement is estimable by experienced marine chemists, (3) competition among qualified vendors will help set honest prices, and (4) there is no research or exploratory element that would make costs unpredictable. The IDIQ wrapper appropriately handles the fact that the government cannot predict exactly how many certifications will be needed across five years. This is a textbook appropriate use of FFP IDIQ — discrete, repeatable professional services with a well-defined deliverable. No square peg concern is present.
