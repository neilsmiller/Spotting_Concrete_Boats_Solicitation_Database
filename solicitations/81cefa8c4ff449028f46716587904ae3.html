Compliance Over Outcomes
Does the contract measure process compliance rather than actual outcomes?
Insufficient Data 0 quotes ▾

⚠ Insufficient content to evaluate this sin — the solicitation lacked a substantive Statement of Work or attachments.

Reasoning
This solicitation (N0038326QSA46) is a simplified acquisition for a physical supply item — a 'Distribution Unit' (NSN/classification code 7G20, NAICS 334290) — posted as a brief Request for Quote with a 6-day response window. The document consists almost entirely of standard FAR/DFARS clauses applicable to supply purchases: warranty provisions, Buy American requirements, IUID valuation, Wide Area Workflow payment instructions, and source approval requirements. There is no Statement of Work, no Performance Work Statement, no Quality Assurance Surveillance Plan, no reporting requirements, and no deliverable schedule beyond the physical item itself. The solicitation contains no QA accountability structure that could be evaluated for compliance-over-outcomes patterns — it is a straightforward supply buy, not a services contract. There is insufficient content to meaningfully assess Sin #2. The warranty clause ('Warranty of Supplies of a Noncomplex Nature, 12 months, 60 days after discovery of defect') and the 'Higher-Level Contract Quality Requirement' clause reference are the only quality-related provisions, and these are standard supply contract terms that represent outcome-oriented acceptance criteria (the product must work), not compliance theater. No finding of this sin is warranted.
Insider Rewards
Do requirements or criteria favor incumbent or well-connected contractors?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is a small, straightforward procurement for a distribution unit (NSN-based supply item) under NAVSUP WSS. The document contains no evaluation criteria, no past performance point systems, no dollar-threshold experience requirements, no federal-specific experience mandates, and no personnel qualification requirements that could constitute insider rewards. The only structural feature that could remotely resemble incumbency bias is the Government Source Approval (GSA) requirement, which is standard for military hardware and electronics components under NAICS 334290. Critically, the solicitation explicitly provides a pathway for non-approved sources to compete by submitting a SAR with their proposal — the process is open, not closed. There are no stacked criteria, no evaluation bonus points for prior similar contracts, and no institutional knowledge requirements. This is a commodity procurement with standard clauses, not a complex services acquisition where insider rewards typically manifest. Severity is assessed at 1 — minimal.

Reasoning
This is a straightforward supply procurement for a discrete hardware item — a Distribution Unit (NSN-based, NAICS 334290, Classification Code 7G20) — from NAVSUP Weapon Systems Support. The note that vendors should inform the PCO if FFP cannot be determined is a standard protective measure, not evidence of instability. No square peg issue is present.
