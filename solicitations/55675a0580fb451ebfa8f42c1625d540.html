Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
Firm-fixed-price is the correct contract type for commercial internet services. The requirement is essentially a utility: a defined level of internet connectivity delivered at a known location over a known period of performance (one base year plus one option year). The scope is stable, the deliverable is routine and commercially available, and GCI's existing infrastructure means there are no technical unknowns that would introduce cost uncertainty requiring a more flexible pricing structure. FAR Part 12 explicitly encourages FFP for commercial items and services, and this acquisition is conducted under those authorities. There is no mismatch between the contract type and the nature of the requirement. The sole-source justification — infrastructure lock-in at a remote Alaskan location — is a separate procurement concern (market competition) and does not implicate the choice of pricing structure. Overall, this is a straightforward and appropriate use of FFP for a commercial, recurring service with predictable costs.
