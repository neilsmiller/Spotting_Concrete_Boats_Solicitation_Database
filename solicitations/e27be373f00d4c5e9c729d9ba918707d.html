Square Peg

Reasoning
This solicitation is for a 1GB commercial telecommunications circuit within Europe — a highly commoditized, well-defined commercial service. The pricing structure (MRC/NRC) is the standard fixed-price model used industry-wide for telecom circuits. The scope is stable and predictable: a circuit with a defined bandwidth is either provisioned or it is not, and the monthly charge is known in advance. FAR Part 12 combined with LPTA evaluation further confirms that this is a straightforward commercial acquisition where fixed-price contracting is not only appropriate but the obvious and correct choice. There is no mismatch between the pricing scheme and the requirements. No square peg issue exists here.
