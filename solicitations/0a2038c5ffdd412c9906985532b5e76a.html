Reasoning

The notice contains no experience requirements, evaluation criteria, personnel qualification standards, or past performance scoring rubrics that could be assessed for insider rewards patterns. There is insufficient detail to assess experience thresholds, evaluation criteria stacking, or institutional knowledge requirements. A meaningful assessment of Sin #4 would require the actual solicitation with evaluation factors, SOW/PWS, and any key personnel or past performance requirements.

Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?

Reasoning
This is a presolicitation notice (notice of intent to sole-source) with no attached Statement of Work, Performance Work Statement, or pricing structure. The notice does not state a contract type, pricing scheme, CLINs, or any labor categories. The only substantive information provided is that the requirement is for an 'ALC/DDC System Software and Maintenance Renewal (Qty 1 Lot)' sole-sourced to Automated Logic/Pacific West Controls Inc. While the nature of the requirement — a software and maintenance renewal — is suggestive of a fixed-price arrangement (renewals of commercial software licenses and maintenance agreements are typically priced on a fixed, per-period basis), there is no explicit or implicit pricing structure provided in the solicitation text from which to assess contract type alignment. Without an attached SOW, PWS, pricing schedule, or any indication of the contract vehicle or type, there is insufficient content to meaningfully evaluate whether a square peg pricing mismatch exists. A renewal of commercial software and maintenance would ordinarily be well-suited to a firm-fixed-price structure, but that inference alone is not sufficient to render a judgment on alignment without confirming what pricing approach is actually being used.
