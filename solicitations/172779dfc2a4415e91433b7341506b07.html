Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a 10GB dedicated circuit — a standard commercial telecommunications service. The contract pricing structure (monthly recurring charges plus non-recurring charges) is effectively a firm-fixed-price commercial service arrangement, which is the correct and industry-standard approach for this type of requirement. Dedicated circuits have well-defined technical specifications (bandwidth, location, service level), predictable monthly costs quoted from commercial rate cards, and a clear install-and-maintain scope. There is no ambiguity about the quantity of labor, the scope of the deliverable, or the duration of the service obligation that would warrant a T&M or cost-reimbursement structure. The FAR Part 12 commercial item framework further confirms the appropriateness of fixed pricing here. The LPTA evaluation methodology is also aligned with a fixed-price commercial service, since the primary differentiator among technically acceptable quotes is price. There is no meaningful mismatch between the pricing structure and the requirement. This is a severity 1 — the contract type is appropriate for what is being bought.
