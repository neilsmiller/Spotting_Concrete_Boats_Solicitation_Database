Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a 100GB Ethernet commercial circuit service (Intra-Europe), procured under FAR Part 12 commercial item procedures. The pricing structure — monthly recurring charges and non-recurring charges — is the industry-standard model for commercial telecommunications services and is effectively a firm-fixed-price arrangement: the government pays a set monthly rate for a defined, continuously delivered service. This is precisely the scenario where FFP is most appropriate: the requirement is well-defined (a specific bandwidth circuit between fixed endpoints), the service is commercially available from multiple providers, the cost and delivery parameters are stable and predictable, and competition through LPTA evaluation will keep pricing honest. There is no ambiguity in scope, no labor-hour variability, and no research or development uncertainty that would argue for T&M or cost-reimbursement. The pricing model aligns well with the nature of the requirement. No square peg issue is present.
