Reasoning
This is a straightforward commercial software license acquisition for Wind River VxWorks 653 RTOS. The use of firm-fixed-price is textbook appropriate here: the requirement is a well-defined commercial product, the deliverable is unambiguous (a software license), and the cost is determinable from vendor pricing lists.
