Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
The solicitation uses Cost-Plus-Fixed-Fee (CPFF), a cost-reimbursement contract type. The requirements are consistent with this choice: the work covers a wide spectrum of engineering support activities including design, development, integration, configuration, testing, troubleshooting, repair, maintenance, and fleet sustainment of naval IC Data Networks. These are inherently variable in nature — particularly troubleshooting and repair work, where the extent and duration of effort cannot be predicted with sufficient certainty to support a fixed-price arrangement. The government is essentially purchasing ongoing engineering expertise to support complex naval systems, not a discrete, well-defined deliverable. CPFF is well-suited to this context: it reimburses actual costs while providing the contractor a fixed fee, giving the government visibility into costs while acknowledging the impossibility of pre-specifying scope with the precision required for FFP or even T&M. The SeaPort-NXG MAC vehicle is specifically structured for professional engineering support services of this type, and CPFF is a standard and widely accepted pricing mechanism for such task orders. There is no meaningful mismatch between the contract type and the requirements as described. Severity 1 is appropriate.
