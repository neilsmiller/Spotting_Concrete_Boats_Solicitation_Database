Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
The solicitation uses Firm-Fixed Price (FFP) for a commercial SaaS patient education platform (Krames on Demand). This is a textbook appropriate use of FFP. SaaS products are sold commercially with established, predictable pricing — typically annual or multi-year subscription fees. The scope is stable and well-defined: access to a content platform with specific named capabilities (content editing, discharge instructions, image library, etc.). There is no variable labor component, no uncertain scope of work requiring T&M, and no complex technical research requiring cost-reimbursement. The Base + 4 Option Years structure is also consistent with how SaaS subscriptions are typically managed in federal contracting, allowing the government to renew at pre-agreed prices. FFP is the correct and standard contract type for commercial software subscriptions. No square peg issue is present here.
