Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a well-defined commercial telecommunications circuit — a 50MB intra-Europe connection — priced on a standard MRC/NRC basis. This is a commodity procurement with a stable, predictable scope: the government knows exactly what it wants (a circuit of specified bandwidth in a defined geography), the market price for such services is readily established through competition among DITCO Basic Agreement holders, and there is no ambiguity in the deliverable. The MRC/NRC pricing structure is functionally equivalent to firm-fixed-price for a recurring service, which is entirely appropriate here. There is no labor-hour uncertainty, no evolving requirements, and no technical complexity that would argue for T&M or cost-reimbursement. The use of LPTA evaluation further confirms the government views this as a commodity where the lowest compliant price wins — consistent with a fixed-price commercial acquisition. The pricing structure aligns well with the requirements. No square peg issue is present.
