Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for the purchase of commercial software licenses (90Meter) from an authorized reseller, procured as a commercial item under FAR Part 12 and FAR 13.5 simplified acquisition procedures. The implied contract type is firm-fixed-price, which is both the regulatory default for commercial item acquisitions (FAR 12.207) and the appropriate choice given the nature of the requirement. Software licenses are discrete, well-defined commercial products with predictable pricing. The scope is stable — the government knows what it wants to buy, and vendors quote a fixed price. There is no labor uncertainty, no evolving requirements, and no technical ambiguity that would warrant T&M or cost-reimbursement pricing. The contract type is well-matched to the requirement. No square peg issue exists here.
