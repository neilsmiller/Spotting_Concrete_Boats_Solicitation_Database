Square Peg

Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for the purchase of a single, commercially available hardware system (GelSight GS Max System) with a specific part number, defined configuration, and a concrete delivery date. The contract type implied by the FAR Part 12 reference is firm-fixed-price commercial item purchase, which is precisely the correct vehicle for this type of requirement. The scope is fully defined, there is no labor uncertainty, no research component, and no ambiguity about what is being delivered. A fixed-price commercial item contract is the canonical best practice for buying an off-the-shelf product. There is no square peg issue here. The severity is 1 — the contract type is well-matched to the requirement.
