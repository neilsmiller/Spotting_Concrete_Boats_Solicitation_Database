Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a commercially available 1Gbps Ethernet circuit lease, procured through DITCO's IQO (Individual Quote Order) vehicle. The pricing structure — monthly recurring charges and non-recurring charges — is the standard commercial model for telecommunications circuit services and is entirely consistent with a firm-fixed-price approach. The requirement is precisely defined: install and maintain a specific bandwidth circuit between two points. There is no labor uncertainty, no evolving scope, and no research or development element. FFP is the appropriate and standard contract type for commercial telecom leases of this kind. LPTA source selection further reinforces that this is a commodity acquisition where price is the differentiating factor. There is no indication of a pricing type mismatch here. Severity is 1 — the contract pricing approach is well-matched to the requirement.
