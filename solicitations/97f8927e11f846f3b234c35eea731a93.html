Compliance Over Outcomes
Does the contract measure process compliance rather than actual outcomes?

⚠ Insufficient content to evaluate this sin — the solicitation lacked a substantive Statement of Work or attachments.

Reasoning
This posting is a pre-solicitation notice (presolicitation) for a quantity of 2 units of a specific NSN part (NSN 7H-5895-016480765), sole-sourced under FAR 6.302-1. The notice contains no Statement of Work, Performance Work Statement, Quality Assurance Surveillance Plan, reporting requirements, deliverables list, or any other contract performance accountability structure. The entire text is a brief notice explaining the sole-source justification (data rights unavailable, uneconomical to reverse engineer), citing DoD-STD-100 source control requirements, and providing standard procedural information about document access and trade agreement clauses. There is no content from which to assess whether QA mechanisms measure process compliance versus outcomes. The acquisition is for a physical spare part (C3IT term extension and replacement parts), not a services contract, so the compliance-over-outcomes sin — which is most relevant to service and IT contracts with reporting cadences and surveillance plans — is unlikely to apply even if full solicitation documents were available. Insufficient content to make a meaningful assessment.
