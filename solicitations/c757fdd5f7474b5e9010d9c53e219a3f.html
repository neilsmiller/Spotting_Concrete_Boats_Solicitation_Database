Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a commercial telecommunications circuit lease — specifically a 1GB Ethernet connection — priced using monthly recurring charges (MRC) and non-recurring charges (NRC). This is the standard commercial pricing model for leased line services and is effectively a firm-fixed-price structure: the government pays a set monthly rate for a defined service level over the contract period. This pricing structure is well-suited to the requirement. The scope is clearly defined (1GB Ethernet, specific locations), the service is commercially available and commodity-like, costs are highly predictable (telecom carriers publish standard tariffs and rates), and performance is objectively measurable (circuit is either up and functioning at the contracted bandwidth or it is not). Fixed recurring pricing gives the government cost certainty and is the industry norm for this type of acquisition. There is no indication of scope uncertainty, evolving requirements, or unpredictable labor that would suggest T&M or cost-reimbursement would be more appropriate. The use of LPTA evaluation further reinforces that this is a straightforward, well-defined commercial service procurement. No square peg issue is present.
