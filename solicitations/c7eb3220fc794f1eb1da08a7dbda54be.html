Requirement Sprawl

⚠ Insufficient content to evaluate this sin — the solicitation lacked a substantive Statement of Work or attachments.

Reasoning
This document is a pre-solicitation notice only — it does not contain a Statement of Work (SOW), Performance Work Statement (PWS), or any attached technical requirements document that would allow meaningful assessment of requirement sprawl. The notice describes the procurement at a high level: the contractor will provide Project Facilitator (PF) services including M&V Report review, project tracking, and meeting minutes reporting over a five-year period. However, there is no enumeration of tasks, no specification of methods or processes, no staffing requirements, and no deliverable details. Without a detailed scope of work, it is not possible to evaluate whether the solicitation prejudges the solution by prescribing how work shall be performed. The only substantive technical requirement mentioned — that the Project Facilitator must be DOE approved — is a credential/qualification constraint, not a process mandate. A full requirement sprawl analysis would require the actual solicitation with its PWS/SOW to be published, which per the notice will be issued directly to EMP2 Inc. rather than posted publicly.
