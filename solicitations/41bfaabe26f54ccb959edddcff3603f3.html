Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation uses a Firm-Fixed-Price contract for the renewal of commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) software licenses — RecurDyn, a multi-body dynamics simulation package. FFP is the ideal contract type for this requirement. The scope is completely defined (specific brand, specific license package per attached specifications), the deliverable is a commercial product with an established market price, the quantity is fixed, and competition among CHESS-authorized resellers will keep pricing honest. There is no labor component, no scope uncertainty, and no risk that costs will be difficult to estimate. The FAR explicitly identifies commercial product purchases as appropriate for FFP. The use of LPTA evaluation further reinforces that this is a commodity-style procurement where price is the primary differentiator. No mismatch exists between the contract type and the requirements. Severity is 1 — Minimal.
