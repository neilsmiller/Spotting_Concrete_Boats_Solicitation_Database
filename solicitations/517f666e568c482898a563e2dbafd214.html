Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for the purchase of a 'Display Unit' — a discrete, physical supply item — under NAICS 333998 (All Other Miscellaneous General Purpose Machinery Manufacturing), classified under FSC 7E20. The contract type is fixed-price, as confirmed by the inclusion of the FAR Inspection of Supplies--Fixed-Price clause. Fixed-price is the standard and appropriate contract type for the acquisition of a defined supply item. The requirement is a tangible deliverable (a unit of hardware), the quantity is implicit (a specific purchase), and the pricing can be established through market competition or catalog pricing. There is no indication of variable labor, evolving scope, or complex research that would make a T&M or cost-reimbursement contract more appropriate. The solicitation is being conducted under Emergency Acquisition Flexibilities (EAF) authority, which reflects urgency but does not alter the appropriateness of fixed-price for a supply purchase. The absence of drawings is noted but does not rise to the level of a contract-type mismatch — it is a separate potential concern about specification adequacy, not pricing structure. Fixed-price for a supply item is the textbook appropriate choice, and there is no square peg issue here.
