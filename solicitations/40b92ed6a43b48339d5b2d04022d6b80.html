Compliance Over Outcomes
Does the contract measure process compliance rather than actual outcomes?

⚠ Insufficient content to evaluate this sin — the solicitation lacked a substantive Statement of Work or attachments.

Reasoning
This posting is a pre-solicitation/Request for Information (RFI) — explicitly stated as market research with 'no acquisition strategy has been determined yet.' The document contains no Statement of Work, Performance Work Statement, Quality Assurance Surveillance Plan, deliverables list, reporting requirements, or any contractual accountability mechanisms. The RFI consists entirely of background context about the VA Enterprise Cloud and a list of open-ended industry questions about cloud strategy, pricing models, AI integration, and procurement approaches. There is no content from which to assess whether the eventual contract will emphasize compliance over outcomes — that structure simply does not exist yet. A meaningful analysis of Sin #2 requires an actual solicitation with defined QA processes, reporting cadences, and performance standards. This document has none of those elements. Assessment is deferred pending release of an actual RFP or draft PWS/SOW.

Requirement Sprawl
Does the scope dictate HOW work is done rather than WHAT must be achieved?

⚠ Insufficient content to evaluate this sin — the solicitation lacked a substantive Statement of Work or attachments.

Reasoning
This document is a Request for Information (RFI) — a market research instrument, not a solicitation with a Statement of Work or Performance Work Statement. The RFI contains no task requirements, no 'shall' statements prescribing how a contractor must perform work, no staffing structures, and no workflow mandates. Meaningful analysis of Requirement Sprawl requires a solicitation with actual performance requirements. This RFI lacks that content entirely. A future RFP may well exhibit this sin, particularly given the complexity of the enterprise cloud environment described and the breadth of topics being researched, but no such determination can be made from the present document.
