Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
CPFF is an appropriate contract type for this requirement. The work involves ongoing engineering and technical support for naval navigation systems (ECDIS-N and NCDS), encompassing both maintenance of operational readiness and integration of technological improvements across in-service and new construction platforms. This type of work is characterized by scope uncertainty: the specific tasks, labor hours, and technical challenges cannot be predicted with sufficient accuracy to support a firm-fixed-price contract, and the quantity of labor is too variable and uncertain for T&M to be clearly superior. CPFF is a reasonable fit for complex, technically uncertain, multi-faceted engineering support of this nature — it reimburses actual costs while providing the contractor a fixed fee, giving the government visibility into expenditures without requiring the government to define a precise deliverable upfront. The use of SeaPort-NXG, a MAC vehicle specifically designed for these types of naval engineering support services, further reinforces that the contract type is appropriate and consistent with established Navy acquisition patterns for similar work. No meaningful mismatch between the pricing structure and the requirements is evident from the available information.
