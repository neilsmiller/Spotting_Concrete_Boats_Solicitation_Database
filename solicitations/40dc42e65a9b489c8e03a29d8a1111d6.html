Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 3 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation presents a straightforward commercial product acquisition: brand-name Getac S510 laptops procured to support the Joint Mission Planning Software (JMPS) program. The contract type — FFP-IDIQ — is well-suited to this requirement. FFP is appropriate because the item being purchased is a specific commercial product with a known configuration, predictable unit pricing, and clear deliverables (physical hardware). An IDIQ structure is also sensible here: the government likely cannot predict exact quantities over a two-year period but knows the general range of need, making FFP-IDIQ a reasonable fit. There is no indication of undefined scope, evolving requirements, or unpredictable labor content that would suggest T&M or cost-reimbursement would be preferable. This is not a square peg; it is an appropriate contract type for a commercial hardware procurement.
