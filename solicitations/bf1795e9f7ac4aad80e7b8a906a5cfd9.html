Reasoning

The evaluation criteria referencing salient characteristics likewise test product conformance, not vendor incumbency history. Severity is assessed at 1 — the solicitation contains no meaningful insider rewards indicators.

Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is a renewal of commercial software licenses, upgrades, and maintenance support for Thermo-Calc — a specific branded product used for thermodynamic calculations. FFP is the correct and standard contract type for this requirement. The deliverables are well-defined (specific line items listed in the salient characteristics attachment), the scope is stable (renewal of existing licenses), and pricing is inherently predictable because it derives from the vendor's commercial catalog. There is no ambiguity in scope, no labor-hour variability, and no research or development uncertainty that would argue for T&M or cost-reimbursement. The FAR specifically endorses FFP for commercial products, and software license renewals are among the clearest cases where FFP is appropriate. No square peg issue exists here.
