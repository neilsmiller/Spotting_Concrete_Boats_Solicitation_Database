Square Peg

Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for the purchase of commercial software licenses (Locklizard DRM perpetual licenses, command-line add-ons, ecommerce add-ons, and version upgrades) plus a formula-driven annual support fee. The pricing structure is clearly firm-fixed-price: every line item has a defined part number, fixed quantity, and either a catalog price or a mathematically determinable price (12% of license fees). This is precisely the scenario where FFP is not only appropriate but required under FAR 12 (commercial items). The requirements are completely well-defined — specific product SKUs, specific user/document/customer counts, specific hosting configuration — leaving no ambiguity about scope, labor content, or deliverables. There is no mismatch between the implied contract type and the nature of the requirements. The base period plus three option periods structure is standard for software maintenance and support and does not introduce any pricing-type concern. No square peg issue exists here.
