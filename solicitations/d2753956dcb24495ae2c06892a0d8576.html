Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for the renewal and maintenance of an existing software system (RTS) under NAICS 513210 (Software Publishers). The product/service code 7A21 references business application software with perpetual licensing, and the structure — one base year plus four option years, each priced as a discrete line item — is consistent with a firm-fixed-price commercial software maintenance agreement. Software maintenance renewals are precisely the kind of requirement where FFP is appropriate: the scope is known (maintaining an existing system), the vendor's pricing is commercially established, and the annual cost can be estimated with reasonable certainty. There is no indication of uncertain scope, variable labor quantities, or R&D-style unpredictability that would argue for T&M or cost-reimbursement. The solicitation uses SF 1449, which is the standard form for commercial item acquisitions, further confirming a fixed-price commercial approach. This is an appropriate pricing structure for the requirement. No square peg issue is present.
