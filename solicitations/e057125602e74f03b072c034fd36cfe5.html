Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a 1GB Ethernet commercial lease — a well-defined, commodity telecommunications service with predictable, stable pricing. The requirement is straightforward: DISA needs a carrier-grade Ethernet circuit delivered between two points (NM and NM), maintained at a defined bandwidth, billed at a fixed monthly recurring rate. This is precisely the use case for fixed-price commercial contracting. The scope is clear (1GB Ethernet circuit), the deliverable is measurable (circuit availability and bandwidth), and commercial carriers quote these services at published or negotiated fixed rates. There is no ambiguity in what needs to be delivered, no labor-intensive service with unpredictable quantities, and no complex R&D. The MRC/NRC pricing structure maps directly to commercial telecommunications billing norms and gives the government full cost certainty over the contract term. The LPTA evaluation method further confirms this is a price-competition exercise for a commodity service. There is no misalignment between the pricing structure and the requirements — this is a textbook application of fixed-price commercial contracting for a utility-like service.
