Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is a straightforward commercial services purchase: satellite airtime and monthly maintenance fees for a defined one-year period from a sole-source provider. FFP is the correct contract type here. The costs are predictable — satellite airtime and maintenance are typically billed at fixed monthly rates, and the government knows exactly what it is buying. There is no scope uncertainty, no variable labor component, and no research or development element that would argue for T&M or cost-reimbursement. The one-year term with recurring monthly fees is precisely the kind of stable, well-defined requirement FAR identifies as appropriate for FFP. There is no misalignment between the contract pricing structure and the requirements. Severity is 1 — minimal.

The requirement is for a specific commercial service from a specific provider. The quantity (airtime and monthly maintenance for one year) is fixed and not subject to fluctuation. There is no ambiguity in scope that would argue for a different contract type.
