Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a 10GB Ethernet leased circuit between two fixed geographic locations — a classic commercial telecommunications service. The pricing model (monthly recurring charges and non-recurring charges) is the industry-standard commercial structure for leased line services and is effectively a fixed-price arrangement: the vendor provides continuous connectivity at an agreed monthly rate, and the government pays regardless of utilization fluctuations. This is entirely appropriate for the requirement. Leased circuit services have stable, predictable costs; the deliverable (a functioning high-bandwidth connection) is well-defined; and there is no ambiguity in scope or labor that would suggest a need for T&M or cost-reimbursement pricing. The use of LPTA further confirms that requirements are sufficiently defined to allow price-based competition among technically acceptable vendors. There is no pricing mismatch here — the commercial MRC/NRC structure is the correct tool for this kind of requirement. Severity is 1.
