Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation uses a personal services contract (PSC) with negotiated hourly rates — functionally a labor-hour arrangement. The requirements are for expert advisory services (government debt management, capital markets, public finance) delivered in dynamic, unpredictable emerging-market environments, with resident assignments that 'are likely to be renewed' and intermittent short-term deployments. The scope of advisory work inherently cannot be pre-specified with the precision needed for fixed-price contracting: the nature of policy advice, the pace of host-country reform, and the duration of engagement all vary by country and circumstance. A labor-hour personal services contract is the textbook appropriate vehicle here — the government is purchasing expert time and judgment, not a defined deliverable. The hourly rate structure provides cost transparency and a ceiling (EX-III equivalent), while the open-and-continuous recruitment mechanism allows OTA to staff assignments as needs arise. There is no meaningful mismatch between contract type and requirements. Severity is 1.
