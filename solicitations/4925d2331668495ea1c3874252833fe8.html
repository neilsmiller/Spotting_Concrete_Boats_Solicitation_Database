Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation presents a textbook FFP procurement scenario. The government is purchasing brand-name commercial CAD/CAM software licenses (SolidWorks and CAMWorks), along with support and training — items that have established commercial pricing, well-defined deliverables, and predictable scope. There is no meaningful uncertainty about what is being purchased or how much it will cost. The vendor knows exactly what they are selling; the government knows exactly what it is buying. FFP is unambiguously the correct contract type here. FAR 12.207 directs use of FFP for commercial item acquisitions, which this explicitly is (issued under FAR Subpart 12.2). The support and training components could theoretically introduce minor scope variability, but for named commercial products these are typically standardized offerings with fixed pricing as well. There is no indication of open-ended labor, undefined deliverables, or evolving requirements that would suggest T&M or cost-reimbursement would be preferable. No square peg concern is present.
