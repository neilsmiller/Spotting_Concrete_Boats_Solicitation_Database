Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Moderate 4 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for BRAC PMO technical and program support services issued against the SeaPort MAC IDIQ vehicle. SeaPort MAC task orders are almost universally structured as labor-hour contracts with fixed hourly rates by labor category — a pricing mechanism that is generally well-suited to advisory and support services work where the type of labor is known but the quantity and timing are not predictable in advance. BRAC program management support fits this profile: staffing requirements fluctuate with base closure activity, policy changes, and congressional direction, making fixed-price contracts difficult to scope accurately. A labor-hour or T&M structure is defensible and expected for this type of work. However, the solicitation provides no attachments — no PWS, SOW, CLIN structure, or explicit statement of contract type. This is a significant analytical limitation. Without the PWS, it is impossible to determine whether the work includes any deliverable-oriented CLINs that might be better suited to fixed-price pricing, or whether the entire contract is structured as open-ended staff augmentation. For BRAC PMO support, there may be discrete, well-defined project components (e.g., specific environmental studies, facility transfer documents, or construction coordination tasks) that could be priced on a fixed-price basis, which would provide better cost control than labor-hour pricing for those elements. Given the SeaPort MAC context and the nature of PMO support services, the presumed labor-hour structure is reasonable and defensible. The severity is assessed as 2 (Moderate) rather than 1 because: (1) there is no explicit confirmation of the contract type, and (2) if any portion of the work involves predictable, deliverable-oriented tasks, a hybrid approach with some FFP CLINs would provide stronger cost discipline. The lack of attachments prevents a definitive lower-severity ruling. If the contract is confirmed to be labor-hour for broad advisory support with no fixed deliverables, severity would drop to 1.
