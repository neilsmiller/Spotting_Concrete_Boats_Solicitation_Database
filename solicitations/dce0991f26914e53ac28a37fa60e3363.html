Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
The contract is Firm Fixed Price (FFP) for a bridge action covering recurring EOD flight support technician services at a single installation. The work described — training scheduling, documentation, equipment and munitions account maintenance, checklist execution, and safety compliance — is essentially steady-state support work performed by a defined set of technicians at a known location. This type of work is well-suited to FFP: the labor categories, hours, and scope are reasonably predictable over a short bridge period (5 months base plus up to 2 one-month options). The government is not asking for R&D, uncertain deliverables, or variable quantities of labor — it is buying a known set of ongoing support functions from an incumbent (R3 Inc) that has already been performing the requirement. The fact that this is a sole-source bridge to a known incumbent further reduces pricing uncertainty, since historical performance data should be available to estimate costs accurately. There is no indication that scope will change materially during the bridge period. FFP is appropriate here and consistent with FAR guidance to use FFP when 'the risk involved is minimal or can be predicted with an acceptable degree of certainty.' No significant square peg concern is present. Severity is assessed as 1 — Minimal.
