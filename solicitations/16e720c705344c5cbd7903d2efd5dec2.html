Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a commodity telecommunications service — a 100 MB dedicated commercial leased circuit within Europe. The pricing structure (MRC/NRC) is the industry-standard fixed-price model for leased circuits, and it aligns well with the nature of the requirement. Fixed-price is the correct approach here: the bandwidth is precisely specified, the service type is commercially available, the requirement is a re-award (meaning the government has prior experience with this exact service), and there is no ambiguity in scope or deliverable. The government will pay a known monthly rate for a known level of service — there is no labor uncertainty, no evolving requirement, and no research dimension that would suggest T&M or cost-reimbursement would be superior. The LPTA evaluation method further reinforces that this is a straightforward commercial acquisition where price competition on a well-defined deliverable is the goal. No square peg concern is present.
