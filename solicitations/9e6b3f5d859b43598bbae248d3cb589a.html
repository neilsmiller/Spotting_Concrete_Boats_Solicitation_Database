Insider Rewards
Do requirements or criteria favor incumbent or well-connected contractors?
Insufficient Data 0 quotes ▾

⚠ Insufficient content to evaluate this sin — the solicitation lacked a substantive Statement of Work or attachments.

Reasoning
This document is a one-page Notice of Intent to Award Sole Source — it is not a full solicitation with evaluation criteria, experience requirements, personnel qualifications, or past performance scoring rubrics. The notice states only that the Air Force intends to award non-competitively to SAIC under FAR 13.106-1(b)(1) for JRE help desk and sustainment services, and invites capability statements. There is no Statement of Work, Performance Work Statement, evaluation criteria section, key personnel requirements, or past performance scoring methodology attached or described. Without these elements, there is no basis to assess whether experience requirements, evaluation criteria, or knowledge expectations function as insider rewards. The sole-source intent is itself notable — the government has already decided SAIC is the only capable source — but that structural decision is distinct from the solicitation design pattern this sin measures (i.e., requirements written to favor an incumbent while nominally allowing competition). A sole-source justification bypasses competition entirely rather than structuring a competition to exclude non-incumbents. Accordingly, there is insufficient content to evaluate Sin #4, and severity is set to 1 by default.
