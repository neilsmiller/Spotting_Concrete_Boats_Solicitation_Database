Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 3 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for commercial software license maintenance renewals — specifically CGTech VERICUT and associated module renewals — acquired under FAR Part 12. The requirement is well-defined: fixed quantities of named software modules over a defined period of performance. There is no uncertainty in scope, labor, or deliverables. Software maintenance renewals of this type are priced commercially at catalog or list rates, making cost estimation straightforward. A firm-fixed-price contract structure is the natural and appropriate fit. FAR 12.207 effectively mandates FFP for commercial item acquisitions except in narrow circumstances not present here. There is no indication of scope ambiguity, unpredictable labor content, or research uncertainty that would justify T&M or cost-reimbursement. While the solicitation does not explicitly state the contract pricing type, the FAR Part 12 commercial acquisition framework and the nature of the line items (discrete, quantity-specified software maintenance renewals) strongly imply FFP. No square peg concern is present here.
