Square Peg

Reasoning
This is a straightforward commercial product/services acquisition. The government needs 331 Dell 7680 laptops retrofitted — a well-defined, countable, discrete task with a known quantity and a specific vendor (Dell Federal Systems). FFP is the appropriate contract type here: the scope is clear, the quantity is fixed, the work is predictable, and Dell — as the OEM — is the authoritative source for pricing and execution. There is no meaningful uncertainty about scope, labor hours, or technical risk that would favor T&M or cost-reimbursement. FFP places cost risk on the vendor, which is appropriate when the deliverable and quantity are this well-specified. No mismatch between contract type and requirements is present.
