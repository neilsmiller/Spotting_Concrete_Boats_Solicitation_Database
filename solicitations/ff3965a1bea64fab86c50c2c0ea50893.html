Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation uses a Firm-Fixed Price contract for cable television, internet, phone, and digital music services — all of which are commercial, subscription-based utility services with well-defined, recurring costs. FFP is the correct and standard contract type for this kind of acquisition. The scope is stable (specific service bundles at a fixed location), the pricing is commercially determinable (Midcontinent Communications offers set rates), and there is no labor-hour uncertainty or scope ambiguity that would favor T&M or cost-reimbursement. The FAR's own guidance supports FFP for commercial services where risk is minimal and can be predicted with certainty. The Base + 4 Option Years structure further supports FFP, as it allows the government to lock in pricing over a defined period with optional extension. There is no meaningful mismatch between the contract type and the requirement. Severity is 1 — minimal.

This procurement is for Cable, Internet, Phone, and Digital Music Services. The Black Hills Health Care System (BHHCS) requires cable TV services at the Fort Meade (FM) Veterans Administration (VA) hospital buildings and the Rapid City Clinic. Additional services required include non-enterprise internet services and DMX broadcast digital music.
The services being purchased are recurring, commoditized utility services. Costs are stable and predictable month-to-month. There is no labor uncertainty, no undefined scope, and no technical complexity that would argue for T&M or cost-reimbursement pricing. FFP is entirely appropriate here.
