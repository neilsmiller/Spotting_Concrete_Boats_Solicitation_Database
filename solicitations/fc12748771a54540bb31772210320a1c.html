Square Peg

Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is a DLA supply procurement for a specific hardware item — a 1U THEMIS server — identified by a manufacturer cage code and part number. The contract is firm-fixed-price, as evidenced by the inclusion of FAR clause 'INSPECTION OF SUPPLIES--FIXED-PRICE (AUG 1996).' FFP is the correct and standard contract type for this type of acquisition: the requirement is a defined commercial product, the scope is fixed (deliver the item), and cost can be reasonably estimated based on market pricing. The solicitation even requests pricing support (invoices, cost breakdowns) to establish fair and reasonable pricing, which is consistent with FFP practice. There is no mismatch between the contract type and the nature of the requirement. This is a textbook FFP supply purchase with no square peg concern.
