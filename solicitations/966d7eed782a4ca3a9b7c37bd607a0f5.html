Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for the provision, installation, and maintenance of an existing 10GB Ethernet lease between Michigan and Ohio — a well-defined, commercially available telecommunications service. The pricing model of monthly recurring charges (MRC) and non-recurring charges (NRC) is the industry-standard structure for telecom leases and is inherently fixed in nature: the government knows exactly what service it is buying, the scope is stable (a specific bandwidth circuit between two known endpoints), and the cost can be predicted with reasonable certainty based on commercial market rates. A firm-fixed-price approach is the textbook correct choice here. There is no indication of uncertain scope, variable labor quantities, or evolving requirements that would justify T&M or cost-reimbursement. The FAR Part 12 framework further reinforces the appropriateness of FFP for this commercial service. While the full solicitation package is not publicly available (restricted to entities with a DITCO Basic Agreement), the description provides sufficient context to conclude that the contract type aligns well with the nature of the requirement. No square peg concern is present.
