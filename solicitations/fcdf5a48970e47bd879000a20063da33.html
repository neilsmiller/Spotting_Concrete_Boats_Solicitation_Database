Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a commercial telecommunications leased-line service — a 60MB Ethernet circuit connecting locations in North Carolina and Virginia. The requirement is precisely the type of commercial service for which firm-fixed-price contracting is most appropriate: the deliverable is clearly defined (a functioning circuit at a specified bandwidth), the scope is stable and not subject to change, costs are commercially predictable (carriers publish standard rates for such services), and performance is objectively measurable (circuit is up and operating at contracted bandwidth or it is not). The pricing structure of monthly recurring charges and non-recurring charges is the standard commercial model for telecom circuits and maps cleanly onto a fixed-price construct. There is no ambiguity in scope that would suggest T&M or cost-reimbursement would be more appropriate. The LPTA evaluation method is also consistent with a fixed-price, commercially-available service where technical requirements can be binary (meets spec or does not) and price drives award. No square peg issue is present here.
