Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a defined, commercial telecommunications service: a 1GB Ethernet circuit leased on the IQO (Integrated Quick Order) contract vehicle. The requirement is a commodity — a specific bandwidth circuit between two VA locations — with a well-understood scope, predictable monthly cost, and standard industry pricing. The pricing structure of monthly recurring charges plus non-recurring charges (for installation) is the universal commercial model for leased circuit services and is entirely appropriate for this type of acquisition. Fixed pricing for a defined network circuit is the correct contract type: the service level is clearly specified (1GB Ethernet), the recurring cost is known in advance, and performance is binary (the circuit works at the contracted bandwidth or it does not). There is no ambiguity in scope that would argue for cost-reimbursement, and there is no unpredictable labor component that would argue for T&M. The LPTA evaluation method also aligns well with a fixed-price commodity procurement where technical differentiation is minimal. This is a textbook example of an appropriate fixed-price structure for a commercial service. No square peg concern is present.
