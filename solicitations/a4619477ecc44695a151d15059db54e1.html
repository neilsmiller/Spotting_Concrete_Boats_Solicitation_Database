This solicitation is for a 64KB commercial business line telecommunications service in Puerto Rico — a highly commoditized, well-defined commercial product with predictable monthly costs and no meaningful scope uncertainty. The pricing structure (monthly recurring charges and non-recurring charges) is the standard commercial model for telecom services and is functionally equivalent to a firm-fixed-price arrangement: the government pays a set rate for a defined service delivered continuously over the contract period. There is no ambiguity about the scope of work, no variable labor component, and no unpredictable cost drivers. The requirements — a specific bandwidth, a specific location, a specific service type — are as stable and well-defined as requirements get. Using a fixed monthly rate structure is entirely appropriate here, consistent with FAR Subpart 12.6 commercial item acquisition. There is no square peg issue: the pricing structure matches the nature of the requirement precisely. LPTA evaluation further reinforces that this is a commodity purchase where price is the distinguishing factor among technically equivalent offerings.
