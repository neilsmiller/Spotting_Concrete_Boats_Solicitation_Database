Square Peg
Is the pricing/contract type mismatched to the nature of the work?
Minimal 2 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation is for a single commercial software license (Keysight N7800A Calibration and Adjustment Software) acquired under FAR Part 12 as a commercial item below the Simplified Acquisition Threshold. The implied contract type is firm-fixed-price, which is entirely appropriate for this requirement. The deliverable is precisely defined (a specific software license from a specific OEM), the cost is determinable in advance, the scope cannot expand, and there is no labor component whose quantity is unpredictable. A fixed-price purchase order or simplified acquisition instrument is the textbook vehicle for this kind of transaction. There is no square peg concern here — the pricing structure is well-matched to the nature of the requirement.
