Reasoning
This posting is a presolicitation notice of intent to sole-source — it contains no evaluation criteria, experience requirements, personnel qualifications, past performance scoring rubrics, or statement of work that would allow meaningful assessment of insider rewards patterns. The document's entire substance is a public notice explaining that NOAA intends to award a sole-source contract modification to Spire Global under 41 U.S.C. 3304(a)(1) (only one responsible source), with a brief rationale centered on the unique technical configuration of Spire's already-launched satellite. While the sole-source structure is by definition exclusionary, that is a separate procurement policy question from the 'insider rewards' pattern this sin targets, which concerns solicitation language that creates the appearance of competition while structurally ensuring the incumbent wins. Without an attached SOW, PWS, or RFP with evaluation criteria, there is insufficient content to assess this sin.
