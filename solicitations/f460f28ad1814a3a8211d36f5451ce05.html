Compliance Over Outcomes
Does the contract measure process compliance rather than actual outcomes?
Minimal 0 quotes ▾
Reasoning
This solicitation, N0038326QA081, is an RFQ for NSN 7R5895015163875 under NAICS 336413 (Other Aircraft Parts and Auxiliary Equipment Manufacturing), Classification Code 7G20 (aircraft components/parts). This is a spare parts/supply procurement — not a services contract — which is the critical context for evaluating this sin. Compliance Over Outcomes is a sin that manifests in services contracting, where QA mechanisms can focus on vendor activities and process adherence rather than mission outcomes. In a parts procurement solicitation, the accountability structure is fundamentally different and inherently outcome-oriented: the vendor either delivers conforming parts or they do not. Acceptance criteria are typically based on whether the part meets the NSN specification, passes inspection, and is delivered on time — these are concrete, measurable outcomes. Reviewing the solicitation content available: this is a 63-page document covering a single NSN supply acquisition. For supply contracts of this type (aircraft parts, NAVSUP WSS), the standard accountability structure consists of delivery schedules, First Article Testing if required, inspection and acceptance provisions (typically FAR 52.246 clauses), and contract data requirements — none of which constitute compliance theater. There are no indications of the reporting infrastructure that characterizes this sin: no weekly status reports, no overlapping reporting cadences, no process documentation requirements, no plan-adherence tracking, and no QA surveillance plan focused on vendor activities rather than product quality. The solicitation's QA framework, to the extent it exists in a parts acquisition, is inherently outcome-focused: did the part arrive, does it conform to spec, does it pass inspection? This is the opposite of the sin being evaluated. I found no evidence of reporting burden, prescribed report formats, excessive meeting requirements, or process-compliance surveillance mechanisms. Severity is assessed as 1 (Minimal). This is not a case where the solicitation is poorly structured around compliance — it is simply a supply acquisition where the accountability structure is appropriately outcomes-based by its nature. No evidence items are warranted because no compliance-over-outcomes patterns were identified.
