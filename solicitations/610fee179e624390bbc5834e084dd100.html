Square Peg

Reasoning
This solicitation is for a commercial telecommunications circuit — a straightforward commodity service with a defined bandwidth and fixed geographic endpoints. The pricing structure (MRC + NRC) is the standard commercial telecom billing model and is functionally equivalent to a fixed-price arrangement: the government pays a set monthly recurring charge for a defined level of service. There is no ambiguity in scope, no variable labor component, and no R&D or developmental uncertainty that would call for cost-reimbursement or T&M pricing. The LPTA evaluation method further aligns with this fixed-price, commodity-service context. The pricing scheme is well-matched to the requirement. No square peg concern is present.
